导读第六届虹桥国际经济论坛“《世界开放报告2023》发布暨国际研讨会”于11月5日下午在国家会展中心（上海）举办。会上发布了最新的世界开放指数。指数显示，全球开放度下降趋势仍在延续。如何扭转逆全球化趋势，缩小国家、行业和地区间加剧的开放度差距？在中央广播电视总台CGTN评论栏目《舆论纵贯线》11月9日首播的节目中，商务部原部长陈德铭，联合国主管经济和社会事务副秘书长李军华，非盟委员会经济发展、贸易、旅游、工业与矿业委员阿尔伯特·穆昌加，达能中国、北亚及大洋洲总裁谢伟博，汇丰集团行政总裁祈耀年，诺贝尔经济学奖得主皮萨里德斯发表了自己的洞见。讨论环节中，节目主持人王冠还邀请嘉宾共话中国为促进开放作出的努力。研讨嘉宾包括：国家创新与发展战略研究会（CIIDS）学术委员会常务副主任、重庆市原市长黄奇帆，国务院发展研究中心副主任隆国强，前海国际事务研究院院长郑永年。DESCRIPTIONThe 6th Hongqiao International Economic Forum (HQF) “Press Release and International Symposium on World Openness Report 2023” was held in the afternoon of November 5 at the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai). The latest World Openness Index was released on the symposium, signaling a continuation of the global trend of declining openness. How to reverse deglobalization and reduce intensified disparities in openness between countries, sectors and regions? In CGTN commentary program “The Hub with Wang Guan” premiered on November 9, we can hear insights from Chen Deming, China's former minister of Commerce; Li Junhua, UN under-secretary-general for economic and social affairs; Albert Muchanga, African Union commissioner for economic development, trade, tourism, industry and minerals; Bruno Chevot, president of Danone for China, North Asia and Oceania; Noel Quinn, group chief executive of HSBC Holdings plc; and Christopher Pissarides, Nobel Memorial Prize laureate in Economics.Also, host Wang Guan looks at China's efforts to promote openness in a panel discussion with Huang Qifan, executive deputy director of the Academic Committee of China Institute for Innovation & Development Strategy (CIIDS), and former Mayor of Chongqing; Long Guoqiang, vice president of the Development Research Centre of China’s State Council (DRC); and Zheng Yongnian, director of the Qianhai Institute for International Affairs.
